The Artist

Hands that turn tradition into art

Every design begins with a conversation — your outfit, your function, your personality. From delicate Arabic strokes to dense, storytelling bridal sleeves, each pattern is drawn freehand and tailored to you, never copy-pasted from a template.

Yuvraj Mehandi Artist works with natural, skin-friendly henna and takes the time intricate bridal work deserves, so the design you fall in love with is the one that stains beautifully and lasts through every celebration.
